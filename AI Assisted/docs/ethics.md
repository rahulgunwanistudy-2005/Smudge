# Ethics and consent

Use only your own images or images provided with informed consent. Do not upload government documents, third-party social media captures, or volunteer photographs into this repository. Built-in examples are procedural textures, prominently labelled synthetic; they are not a person's identity.

The app provides no print-crop download, minutiae download or physical-clone workflow. JSON exports remove minutiae coordinates. The evaluation lab only compares controlled synthetic or privately consented inputs. Do not extend it into a search tool for identifying people.

Consent is not a checkbox alone: the person must understand what fingerprint processing entails, where originals are held and how to withdraw. Never claim protection or an invisible edit based only on our synthetic tests. Never backdate work for competition eligibility.
