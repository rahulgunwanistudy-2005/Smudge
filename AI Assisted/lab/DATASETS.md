# Datasets and permissions

Only `pipeline/synthetic.py` output is used in the committed evaluation. It is procedural ridge-like texture with no human subject, generated by the project. It is not an Anguli-generated biometric dataset and is not evidence for human contactless efficacy. Seeds, dimensions, generator and result JSON are committed; no human raw image is present.

SOCOFing, FVC2002/FVC2004, and Anguli were suggested in the build brief but have not been obtained or evaluated. Their licences and redistribution terms must be reviewed before use. They are not claimed as training or evaluation data here.

Consented contactless data at 0.3/1/2/3m is not available. Record consent outside Git, keep raw data outside the repository, and commit only aggregate metrics after study. Do not fill absent distance charts with invented values.
